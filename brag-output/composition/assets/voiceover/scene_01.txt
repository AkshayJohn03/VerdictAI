Companies today use one A I model to grade the answers of another: rate this reply; which answer is better? That grader is called a judge. But who grades the grader? The judge is itself a machine, with habits. It may prefer whichever answer it read first. It may favor longer answers, mistaking length for substance. It may be kinder to writing that sounds like its own. Verdict A I is the quality-control lab for that grader: an exam grader that first learns from human teachers.
