Thirty-second recap. The judge grades with a written rubric, not vibes: anchored criteria, quoted evidence. Comparisons run in both orders, so position bias is caught and counted, along with verbosity and self-preference. Kappa measures agreement beyond luck; our example is zero point five. Isotonic regression curves judge scores onto the human scale, by hand with PAV. Golden datasets are generated on a coverage grid, decontaminated, and versioned with hashes. And the gate separates real regressions from noise, blocking only the real ones. That is Verdict A I: nothing is trusted without a number.
