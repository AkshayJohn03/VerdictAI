Next, the exam itself. A golden dataset is a fixed, versioned set of test questions with known-good answers. The generator builds variety on purpose: topics, personas, difficulty, and nasty edge cases like hidden instructions. A coverage matrix marks every cell of the grid — a test asserts no empty cells. The dataset ships like code: a JSON-L file, a manifest of S H A 256 hashes, a changelog. Then, decontamination: any exam question sharing eight consecutive words with the corpus the model may have memorised is dropped. Eight words in a row is a quote, not a coincidence. Don't let the exam leak into the textbook.
