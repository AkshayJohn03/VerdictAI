The judge says five; the humans say three point five. It is not evil — it is miscalibrated, and it needs its grades curved. Isotonic regression draws the best mapping from judge scores to human scores under one rule: higher must never become lower. No shape is assumed — it captures saturation at the top and dead zones at the bottom. The algorithm is pool-adjacent-violators: walk left to right; whenever a block dips below its neighbor, pool them and average until the staircase only climbs. The report prints agreement before and after, so overfitting shows itself. Platt scaling fits one rigid sigmoid — real judge error is messier.
