How do we know the judge grades like a human? Humans grade a sample too. Counting exact agreement lies: two coin-flipping graders still agree a quarter of the time. Cohen's kappa measures agreement beyond luck: observed agreement minus chance agreement, over one minus chance agreement. Zero is a coin flip; one is perfect. The worked example, pinned by a test: the judge grades zero, zero, one, one; the human grades zero, one, one, one. Observed agreement, three quarters. Chance agreement, one half. Kappa: zero point five — moderate. Weighted kappa gives partial credit on five-point scales; Spearman checks the ranking.
